Delete your account

You can delete your Primal Strength account and all its data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Primal Strength and tap the gear (Settings) at the top right.
  2. Scroll to Privacy and tap Delete my account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm.

This works the same in the Android app and the web app.

What gets deleted

  • Your login and profile.
  • All workouts, cardio, check-ins, DEXA scans, health reports and consent records.
  • Your leaderboard summary, buddy links, high-fives and challenge entries.
  • For trainers: your invite code, challenges, client groups, notes and alerts. For gym owners: your gym and gym code.
  • The copy of your data on the phone you delete from.

Your trainer is told you've left, and their notes about you are deleted the next time they open the app. Google removes any remaining copies on its own schedule.

Can't open the app?

Email mmuralidharan111@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Primal Strength account". We'll delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.
