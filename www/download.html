Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If asked, allow installs from your browser or Files app.
  3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, tap More details → Install anyway. It's a test build, not on the Play Store yet.
  4. Open Primal Strength and create your account.

Already installed? Download again to update — your data is kept.

On iPhone

  1. Open the web app link above in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the gorilla icon and create your account.
