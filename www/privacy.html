Privacy policy

Version 1.0 · effective 7 November 2026

Primal Strength is a workout app for people, their trainers and their gyms. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, who can see it, and how you control it. It is written with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind.

Who we are

Primal Strength is run by Manas Muralidharan, Bengaluru, India, who decides how your data is used (the "data fiduciary" under the DPDP Act). For any privacy question, request or complaint, email mmuralidharan111@gmail.com.

Who can use the app

Primal Strength is for adults aged 18 and over. We ask for your date of birth when you start and don't let anyone younger continue.

What we collect

DataExamples
AccountName, email address, password (handled by Google Firebase; we never see it), whether you're a client, trainer or gym owner.
ProfileDate of birth, sex (optional), height, weight, goal, training experience, schedule, equipment, and your city and country (optional).
TrainingYour program, workouts, sets, reps and weights, cardio sessions, check-ins (body weight, waist, sleep, recovery), points, streaks, badges and personal bests.
Health data (only with your separate consent)DEXA scan results, health reports (blood work, blood pressure, VO2 max and other results you enter), the answers to the pre-exercise health screening, and pain or injury information, including back-pain scores.
SocialYour trainer link, buddies, high-fives, challenges you join, and whether you appear on your coach's weekly board.
Consent recordsWhen you agreed to this policy and to health data, and which version.

Report files you attach (PDFs and photos of DEXA or blood reports) stay on your phone and are not uploaded. We don't use advertising, sell data, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

Location

You can type your city and country, or tap Use my location. If you do, your phone's location is used once to look up your city: the coordinates are sent to BigDataCloud's reverse-geocoding service, which returns the city and country. We save only the city and country, never your coordinates. We use them to show units (kg or lb, km or miles) and local milestones.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and keep your log in sync across devices.
  • To build and adjust your training plan, targets and progress charts.
  • To let your trainer coach you, and your gym see how its members are doing in summary.
  • For challenges, buddies and leaderboards you choose to join.
  • With your health consent: to factor scans, test results, screening answers and pain into your training.

We use your data only for these purposes, based on your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time (see "Your choices").

Who can see your data

Your trainer's private notes about you are the trainer's records. Your data is not shared with anyone else unless the law requires it.

Where it's stored and how it's protected

Your account and log are stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud), which may keep data on servers outside India. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and Google encrypts it at rest. Database rules limit each person to the data described above, and those rules are tested automatically with every app update. A copy is also kept on your phone so the app works offline; protect your phone with a screen lock.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, it is deleted from our database straight away; Google removes any remaining copies on its own schedule. If you switch off health data, your DEXA scans, health reports, screening answers and pain scores are deleted. If we ever stop running the app, we'll tell you and delete the data.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data: everything is visible in the app; edit your profile in Settings → Edit my profile, and edit or delete individual entries.
  • Export it: Settings → Export (.json or .csv).
  • Withdraw health consent: Settings → Privacy → Health data.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Privacy → Delete my account, or see how to delete your account.
  • Nominate someone to act for you if you die or can't act yourself: email us their name and contact details.
  • Complain: email us first. We'll reply within 7 days and aim to resolve it within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Not medical advice

Primal Strength gives general fitness guidance. It is not medical advice and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if the health screening flags anything, and stop if you feel chest pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects how your data is used, the app will ask you to review and agree again before you continue.
