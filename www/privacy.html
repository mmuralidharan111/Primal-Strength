Privacy policy

Version 1.3 · effective 25 November 2026 (see "Changes" at the end)

Primal Strength is a workout app for people, their trainers and their gyms. This policy explains what personal data the app collects, why, who can see it, and how you control it. It is written with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind.

Who we are

Primal Strength is run by Manas Muralidharan, Bengaluru, India, who decides how your data is used (the "data fiduciary" under the DPDP Act). For any privacy question, request or complaint, email mmuralidharan111@gmail.com.

Who can use the app

Primal Strength is meant for adults. We ask for your date of birth (optional) to set safe training targets. If you are under 18, use the app only with a parent's or guardian's permission. If we learn that a child's data was collected without that permission, we will delete it.

The app's code and fonts come from our own website and app files; it doesn't load scripts, fonts or trackers from other companies' servers.

Location

You can type your city and country, or tap Use my location. If you do, your phone's location is used once to look up your city: the coordinates are sent to BigDataCloud's reverse-geocoding service, which returns the city and country. We save only the city and country, never your coordinates. We use them to show units (kg or lb, km or miles) and local milestones.

Why we use it

  • To run your account and keep your log in sync across devices.
  • To build and adjust your training plan, targets and progress charts.
  • To let your trainer coach you, and your gym see how its members are doing in summary.
  • To notice when you're training less than your own usual pattern, so your trainer can check in with you, and to show your gym how well members keep training.
  • For challenges, buddies and leaderboards you choose to join.
  • With your health consent: to factor scans, test results, screening answers and pain into your training.

We use your data only for these purposes, based on your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time (see "Your choices").

Where it's stored and how it's protected

Your account and log are stored with Google Firebase (Google Cloud), which may keep data on servers outside India. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and Google encrypts it at rest. Database rules limit each person to the data described above, and those rules are tested automatically with every app update. A copy is also kept on your phone so the app works offline; protect your phone with a screen lock. The Android app keeps its data out of Android's cloud backups and phone-to-phone transfers; when you change phones, sign in and your log comes back from your account (or, without an account, use Settings → Save full backup).

The website, download page and this policy are hosted on GitHub Pages, and the Android app is downloaded from GitHub (GitHub, Inc., part of Microsoft). Like any web host, GitHub records visitors' IP addresses in its logs for security; we don't receive them. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, it is deleted from our database straight away; Google removes any remaining copies on its own schedule. If you switch off health data, your DEXA scans, health reports, screening answers and pain scores are deleted. If we ever stop running the app, we'll tell you and delete the data.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data: everything is visible in the app; edit your profile in Settings → Edit my profile, and edit or delete individual entries.
  • Export it: Settings → Export (.json or .csv).
  • Withdraw health consent: Settings → Privacy → Health data.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Privacy → Delete my account, or see how to delete your account.
  • Nominate someone to act for you if you die or can't act yourself: email us their name and contact details.
  • Complain: email us first. We'll reply within 7 days and aim to resolve it within 30 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Not medical advice

Primal Strength gives general fitness guidance. It is not medical advice and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if the health screening flags anything, and stop if you feel chest pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects how your data is used, the app will ask you to review and agree again before you continue.

Version 1.3 (25 November 2026) adds the gym web app. Gyms that use Primal Strength can keep their membership records in it (name, phone, plan, payments, receipts, desk check-ins and why a member left), including for members who don't use the app. The gym's owner and its front-desk staff see these records; trainers and other gyms don't. If you use the app, your gym owner can link your membership record to your account. Your gym decides what it records about you, so contact your gym about those records first; we store them for the gym and can help if needed. If you delete your app account you can optionally tell your trainer why. Nothing changes about how your training data is used, so you aren't asked to agree again.

Version 1.2 (24 November 2026) adds the retention features. You can add a WhatsApp number (optional; only your trainer sees it) and a membership end date. You or your trainer can pause your training; your gym sees the pause dates, never the reason. The app compares your recent training with your own usual pattern, and your trainer can record follow-ups (Called, Back training or Lost), which your gym owner also sees. These use the training data you already log, so you aren't asked to agree again.

Version 1.1 (24 November 2026) describes the app more fully; it doesn't use your data for anything new, so you aren't asked to agree again.
